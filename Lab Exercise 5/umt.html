About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a premier public university in Malaysia focusing on marine science and aquatic resources. Located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, the institution has evolved from a small center into a specialized university dedicated to exploring, managing, and sustaining marine wealth and tropical resources.

UMT offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to equip students with strong technical and practical competencies, driving innovation in maritime informatics, oceanography, and coastal technology.
